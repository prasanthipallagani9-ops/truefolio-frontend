# TRUEFOLIO – Interview Notes

## Interview 1: Course Coordinator

- **Date:** 16 September 2026
- **Person interviewed:** Mr. Naveen 
- **Role:** Course Coordinator, SRM University AP
- **Participants:** Prasanthi Pallagani and Ujvala Bandi
- **Interview stage:** Initial idea discussion, before further project development

### Purpose of the Discussion

We approached our course coordinator to explain our TRUEFOLIO idea and seek suggestions, feedback and possible improvements.

### Problem Discussed

We explained the challenge of limited faculty visibility into students' overall progress, activities and development.

### Feedback and Suggestions Received

The coordinator suggested incorporating AI capabilities into the platform.

His suggestions included:

1. **AI-based recommendations:** Providing students with relevant suggestions based on their existing work and activities. For example, recommending suitable hackathons or internship opportunities based on their progress and interests.
2. **AI chatbot:** Including a chatbot-like feature to help students interact with the platform and receive guidance.

### Key Takeaways

- We gained a better understanding of the problem from a faculty point of view.
- We recognised that the platform could go beyond simply maintaining student activity records.
- AI-based recommendations could help students identify relevant opportunities based on their development.

### Impact on Project Direction

The discussion did not completely change our original idea, but it helped us understand the faculty perspective more clearly. It also introduced the possibility of adding AI-powered recommendations and a chatbot as future enhancements.
