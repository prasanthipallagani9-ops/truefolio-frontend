# TRUEFOLIO – Problem Statement

## Project Title
TRUEFOLIO – Student Development Intelligence Platform

## Problem Statement

Students participate in a wide range of academic and extracurricular
activities, including internships, competitive programming, research,
hackathons, projects, certifications, open-source contributions and
leadership initiatives.

However, information about these achievements is often scattered
across different platforms, documents and personal records. This
makes it difficult for students to maintain a consolidated record
of their development and supporting evidence.

Faculty members may also lack a single, structured view of students'
activities and achievements, making it harder to review submitted
information and track student development.

## Target Users

1. Students who want to maintain a consolidated record of their
   academic, technical and extracurricular development.
2. Faculty members who need to review student activities and
   supporting evidence.

## Proposed Solution

TRUEFOLIO is a student-development intelligence platform that allows
students to record activities, maintain project information and
submit supporting evidence. Faculty members can review submitted
activities and approve them, request changes or reject them.

## Core Objective

To provide a structured platform that improves the visibility,
documentation and review of student development.

## Project Scope

The current prototype demonstrates student and faculty workflows,
including activity management, evidence handling, project records,
student profiles and faculty reviews.

The current deployed version uses browser-based demo storage.
Live authentication and shared database integration are not yet
connected.
